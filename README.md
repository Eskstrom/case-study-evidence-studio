# Case Study Evidence Studio

## Product brief

A structured writing workspace that helps product teams turn project notes into credible case studies. It separates confirmed facts, assumptions, evidence links, outcomes, and claims that still need verification.

## Why this belongs in your portfolio

It uses the strength of your own resume/story bank: rigorous evidence, outcome framing, and honest treatment of uncertainty. It is also useful for PMs, founders, and job seekers.

## MVP

- Case-study editor with sections for problem, decision, execution, evidence, and outcome.
- Claim status: confirmed, needs verification, or excluded.
- Evidence checklist and missing-information prompts.
- Export to Markdown.

## Success measures

- A user can identify unsupported claims before publishing.
- Exported case studies retain evidence references and uncertainty.

## Suggested stack

Next.js, TypeScript, local-first storage or Supabase.
